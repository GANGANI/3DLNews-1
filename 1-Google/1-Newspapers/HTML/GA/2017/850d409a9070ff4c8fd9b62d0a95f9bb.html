Weston laid to rest in Arlington National Cemetery


By ohtadmin
on May 31, 2017







 

The 3rd U.S. Infantry Regiment (The Old Guard) participates in the graveside service for U.S. Army 1st Lt. Weston C. Lee in Arlington National Cemetery, Arlington, Va., May 25, 2017. Lee was interred in Section 60 with full military honors. At right, Staff Sgt. E.J. Ramos renders taps at the grave of 1st Lt. Weston Lee of the 82nd Airborne Division’s 2nd Brigade Combat Team.
